# TechTinker Lab Landing Page Implementation Plan

Create a simple, professional landing pa## Goalge for TechTinker Lab that introduces the business, explains its services, and encourages potential clients to get in touch for project-based work.

## Core Positioning
TechTinker Lab is a UK-based business focused on:
- Software development
- Web and app development
- Digital solutions
- STEM curriculum design
- Project-based client work

## Target Audience
- Small businesses needing websites, apps, or custom software
- Schools, clubs, and education providers needing STEM curriculum support
- Clients looking for project-based technical help
- Organisations seeking practical digital solutions

## Landing Page Structure

### 1. Hero Section
Purpose: immediately explain what TechTinker Lab does.

Content:
- Heading: **Build Practical Tech Solutions with TechTinker Lab**
- Subheading: **Software development, web solutions, and STEM curriculum design for project-based client work**
- Call-to-action buttons:
  - **Get in Touch**
  - **View Services**

### 2. About Section
Purpose: briefly introduce the company.

Content:
TechTinker Lab is a UK-based business specialising in software development, digital solutions, and STEM education projects. The company supports clients through project-based engagements in software engineering, websites, apps, systems, and curriculum development.

### 3. Services Section
Purpose: make service offerings clear.

Service cards:
- **Software Development**
  - Custom software solutions
  - Automation tools
  - Technical problem-solving

- **Web & App Development**
  - Landing pages
  - Business websites
  - Web apps and simple digital products

- **STEM Curriculum Design**
  - Coding workshops
  - STEM learning materials
  - Project-based educational content

- **Technical Consulting**
  - Early-stage project planning
  - Solution design
  - Development support

- **GIS & Location-Based Solutions**
  - Interactive maps and spatial data visualisation
  - Location-aware web and mobile apps
  - GIS data processing and analysis

### 4. How We Work Section
Purpose: explain the process.

Steps:
1. Discuss project goals
2. Define scope and deliverables
3. Build and refine the solution
4. Deliver a practical outcome

### 5. Why Choose TechTinker Lab
Purpose: build trust.

Points:
- Project-based and flexible
- Strong focus on practical outcomes
- Combines technical development with educational expertise
- Suitable for both business and learning-focused projects

### 6. Contact Section
Purpose: encourage enquiries.

Content:
- Short message inviting collaboration
- Email contact
- LinkedIn profile
- Optional contact form

Suggested text:
**Interested in working together? TechTinker Lab welcomes enquiries for software development, digital projects, and STEM curriculum design.**

## Visual Style
Keep the design simple and modern:
- Clean white or light background
- One accent colour such as blue or teal
- Clear sans-serif font
- Simple card-based layout
- Generous spacing
- Mobile-friendly responsive structure

## Suggested Tech Stack
For a simple version:
- HTML
- CSS
- JavaScript

For a more scalable version:
- Next.js
- Tailwind CSS
